EL PASO, ELSEWHERE VR  v0.1.12
=============================
VR mod for "El Paso, Elsewhere" (Unity 2021.3.21f1, IL2CPP x64, HDRP, Rewired) - BepInEx 6 be.788.

<img width="766" height="1024" alt="El Paso, Elsewhere" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a321fc1c-8294-477c-97e4-818bb3833d33" />

Views (hold the right stick click 0.8 s to switch; a short click is still R3)
-----
First person (default): you are James - the view is at his head (his head and arms are hidden), the guns are on your
  controllers (6DOF): the right gun in your right hand, the left gun (two pistols) in your left. Where you look is where he looks and walks; the
  guns shoot along their barrel (red aim laser).
Third person: the view is the game's camera behind him. You turn with the right stick, he aims and shoots where
  your right controller points (red laser).

Controls (the VR controllers are an Xbox controller for the game)
-
  Left stick ......... move
  
  Right stick left / right .. turn (snap\smooth)
  
  Right stick up ..... Y
  
  Y (left controller) ....... d-pad up
  
  Triggers ........... LT / RT
  
  Grips ..................... LB / RB
  
  A B X, L3 .......... as on the Xbox pad
  
  R3 (short click) .......... R3
  
  A + B together ..... Start
  
  X + Y together ............ Back
  
  Tap both stick clicks ........ re-centre the view
  
  Hold both stick clicks ....... VR settings menu (stick = choose / change, A = select, B = close)
Menus, cut-scenes: a normal Xbox controller (right stick = right stick).

Two pistols (first person): RT fires the right gun, LT the left gun (both held = taking turns); the left trigger's own
action is on right stick down meanwhile ([Arms] TriggerPerGun).
The guns' place in your hands: VR settings menu > "Adjust the RIGHT / LEFT gun on the controller" - hold the other
hand's grip to grab and move / turn that gun (the other gun stays where it is meanwhile), A = done (saved),
B = back to default.
